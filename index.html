I love to dance. I am learning Kuchipudi. I started dancing since i was 6 years old. In kuchipudi there are 5 different "JATHI", thus there are different steps in each JATHI. Here are the list of JATHIS:

  • CHATHURASHRAM : 4 OR 8 BEATS
  • THISHRAM : 3 OR 6 BEATS
  • MISHRAM : 7 BEATS
  • KHANDAM : 5 BEATS
  • SANKEERNAM : 9 BEATS
